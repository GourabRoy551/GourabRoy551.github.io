§ 00 Why I write here

This is a space for things that do not fit neatly on a CV: how I think about problems, what I am currently learning, what surprised me in a paper, and what it is like to study and do research across multiple countries and programs.

Posts span research notes (papers, models, datasets, and evaluation), learning logs from computer vision and biomedical AI coursework, and student-life updates from the IPCVAI journey across universities and cities.
