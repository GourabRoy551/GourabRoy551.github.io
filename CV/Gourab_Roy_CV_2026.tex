\section{Current Research}
\project{BERT vs CLIP with Whole-Word Partition SHAP}{TRDP 2, Bordeaux, 2026}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Built a gated, resumable experiment comparing a fine-tuned BERT classifier with a frozen zero-shot CLIP text encoder on the same 500 balanced SST-2 sentences, producing 1,000 model-sentence explanation records.
  \item Evaluated classification, SHAP additivity, deletion faithfulness, lexical rationale proxies, bootstrap confidence intervals, and cross-model agreement. BERT achieved 0.936 macro-F1 versus 0.702 for CLIP; all 1,000 explanations passed additivity checks.
  \item Found limited explanation agreement between models (mean Spearman $\rho=0.237$) and stronger SHAP-ranked deletion evidence than random deletion, especially for BERT.
\end{itemize}
