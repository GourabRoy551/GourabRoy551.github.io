BERT vs CLIP with Whole-Word Partition SHAP

Do a fine-tuned BERT and a zero-shot CLIP text encoder rely on the same words? A reproducible SHAP comparison on 500 SST-2 sentences.

Text BERT / CLIP Partition SHAP Faithfulness
Full methodShow less

Reproducible comparison of a fine-tuned BERT classifier and frozen zero-shot CLIP text encoder on the same 500 balanced SST-2 sentences. Generated 1,000 explanation records for NEG, POS, and decision-margin outputs, with additivity, deletion, rationale-proxy, agreement, bootstrap, and runtime checks. BERT reached 0.936 macro-F1 versus 0.702 for CLIP; both models passed every additivity check.

SiteGuard AI - PPE Detection for Construction Safety

Five-class PPE detection with worker-to-PPE matching, comparing YOLO26n/s and YOLOv8n, served through FastAPI and Docker.

Frame YOLO PPE match API
0.878mAP50

Leading the ML part of a team project: training and comparing YOLO26n/s and YOLOv8n detectors for five-class PPE detection (person, helmet, vest, no-helmet, no-vest) with worker-to-PPE matching, served through FastAPI and Docker. The YOLO26n model reached 0.906 precision, 0.835 recall, and 0.878 mAP50 on the held-out test set, packaged with image and video runners, structured JSON outputs, model checksums, a model card, and 27 automated tests.

BanglaCaption40K: Bengali Image-Text Retrieval Benchmark

A Bengali image-text benchmark of 43,868 images and 217,526 captions, with parameter-efficient CLIP fine-tuning.

Image + caption CLIP Retrieval
17.64I→T R@1 (from 6.17)

Built a benchmark with 217,526 captions and 43,868 images. Lightweight CLIP fine-tuning (0.33% of the parameters) raised image-to-text R@1 from 6.17 to 17.64 and text-to-image R@1 from 1.59 to 11.26, with token ablation, patch perturbation, and error analysis.
