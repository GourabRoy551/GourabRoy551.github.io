TRDP 2 · University of Bordeaux · 2026

BERT vs CLIP with Whole-Word Partition SHAP

Reproducible comparison of a fine-tuned BERT classifier and frozen zero-shot CLIP text encoder on the same 500 balanced SST-2 sentences. Generated 1,000 explanation records for NEG, POS, and decision-margin outputs, with additivity, deletion, rationale-proxy, agreement, bootstrap, and runtime checks. BERT reached 0.936 macro-F1 versus 0.702 for CLIP; both models passed every additivity check.

02
Team software project · Bordeaux · Sep 2026

SiteGuard AI - Real-Time PPE Detection

Trained and packaged a five-class YOLO26n detector for person, helmet, vest, no-helmet, and no-vest events. The held-out test set produced 0.906 precision, 0.835 recall, and 0.878 mAP50. The local inference package adds worker-to-PPE association, image and video runners, structured JSON outputs, model checksums, a model card, and 27 automated tests.
