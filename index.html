Gourab Roy

AI master's student in image processing and computer vision, building vision models that can be explained and trusted.

I am an Erasmus Mundus IPCVAI master's student at Université de Bordeaux, working on explainable AI for text and vision-language transformers, medical image analysis, object detection, 3D vision, and multimodal retrieval. Before that, I spent two years as a research assistant at Sylhet Engineering College and co-authored 12 peer-reviewed papers. I am looking for a 5–6 month computer vision internship from February 2027.
