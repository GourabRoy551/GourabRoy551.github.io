01Current project

What do attention and perturbation explanations reveal about BERT and CLIP?

Question
Do attention-based explanations (RFEM, Ayyar et al., 2025) stay faithful beyond Vision Transformers?
Key finding
Attention-sink tokens can hide the words that actually drive BERT and CLIP predictions.
Method
Compare with whole-word Partition SHAP, deletion tests, rationale proxies, and cross-model agreement on 500 balanced SST-2 sentences.

Supervised by Prof. Jenny Benois-Pineau (Université de Bordeaux) and Prof. Marcos Escudero-Viñolo (Universidad Autónoma de Madrid).

Active TRDP
Attention (RFEM) vs Partition SHAP for BERT and CLIP
Core models
BERT, CLIP, ViT, CNNs, YOLO, Siamese networks
Primary data
Text, image-text pairs, CT/MRI, multi-view images, and safety video
Research lens
Attention rollout, SHAP, LIME, faithfulness, detection, 3D vision
