Research areas

Explainable vision and language models for medical and safety-critical workflows.

My research direction is centered on computer vision systems that are useful beyond benchmark scores: clear data assumptions, measurable reliability, and explanations that help people understand when a model should or should not be trusted.

Current direction

Testing how BERT and CLIP explain the same language decisions.

My current TRDP 2 work compares a fine-tuned BERT classifier with a frozen CLIP text encoder on the same 500 balanced SST-2 sentences. Whole-word Partition SHAP, deletion tests, rationale proxies, and cross-model agreement turn explanation quality into something measurable rather than purely visual.
