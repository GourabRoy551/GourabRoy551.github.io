Before joining IPCVAI, I spent two years as a Research Assistant and Adjunct Lecturer in Computer Science and Engineering at Sylhet Engineering College. I designed models, ran experiments, and prepared manuscripts across medical AI, document AI, agricultural vision, NLP, and structural computer vision, work that led to 12 peer-reviewed publications. I also mentored thesis students and helped build new image datasets such as BanglaFont45 and Bangladeshi rice varieties.

I have also served as a reviewer for Informatics in Medicine Unlocked, which helped sharpen how I read research: not only looking for strong results, but also asking how carefully the problem is framed, how honestly the model is evaluated, and whether the method can be trusted outside a clean benchmark.

02 / Explainability

Attention vs perturbation explanations

Do attention maps show what BERT and CLIP really use? Attention-sink tokens say: not always.

RFEM Partition SHAP BERT CLIP
Read moreShow less

My TRDP studies how attention-based explanations such as RFEM behave beyond Vision Transformers. I found that attention-sink tokens can hide the words that drive BERT and CLIP predictions, and I compare them with perturbation-based Partition SHAP instead of treating a heatmap as proof by itself.
